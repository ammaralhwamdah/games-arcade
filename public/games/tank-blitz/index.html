Tank Blitz - Free Online Tank War Game

Tank Blitz is a free browser tank war game where you defend the eagle base at the bottom of a walled arena against waves of enemy tanks. Drive with the arrow keys or WASD, rotate to face a target and fire with Space. Every brick wall can be shot apart, steel blocks stop ordinary shells, water and thick foliage break enemy line of sight, and a flashing carrier tank always drops a power-up when you destroy it - shields, rapid fire, heavy bombs, spare lives and cannon upgrades that let you punch through steel.

Each wave sends faster scouts, tougher heavy tanks and carrier tanks that hunt you down, and every few waves the arena is rebuilt into a new battlefield with tighter corridors. Score points for every tank you scrap, keep your three lives, and do not let a single enemy shell touch the eagle.
